Natural feature extraction is the foundation of the photogrammetry process. It identifies distinctive points in images that can be reliably located across multiple photographs
\Common feature extraction algorithms include[cite: 1]:

| Algorithm | Strengths | Weaknesses | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SIFT** | Scale and rotation invariant[cite: 1] | Computationally expensive[cite: 1] | High-quality, general-purpose reconstruction[cite: 1] |
| **SURF** | Faster than SIFT[cite: 1] | Less accurate than SIFT[cite: 1] | Quick prototyping[cite: 1] |
| **ORB** | Very fast, no patent restrictions[cite: 1] | Less robust to viewpoint changes[cite: 1] | Real-time applications[cite: 1] |
